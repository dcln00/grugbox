In a landmark announcement that has set the West African entertainment industry abuzz, Afrobeats icon David Adeleke—**Davido**—has revealed plans for a historic three-night residency at the newly renovated National Theatre in Iganmu, Lagos, scheduled for December 17–19, 2026.

Titled *'The Homecoming: 30BG at the Monument'*, the residency marks the first time an active contemporary African pop superstar has booked a multi-night residency at Nigeria's premier cultural architectural landmark.

### Revitalizing a Cultural Monument

The National Arts Theatre, built in 1976 ahead of the legendary FESTAC '77, has recently undergone a multi-million dollar state-of-the-art restoration. Davido’s residency represents a symbolic bridge connecting Nigeria's golden cultural heritage with its contemporary global pop supremacy.

"I’ve sold out the O2 in London, Madison Square Garden in New York, and State Farm Arena in Atlanta," Davido said at a packed Lagos press conference. "But performing in the heart of Lagos, inside the monument where our cultural icons stood in 1977, is the greatest honor of my life."

### What Fans Can Expect: Big Band Arrangements & Intimate Staging

Unlike standard arena spectacles, the National Theatre residency will feature a bespoke, intimate 3,500-seat theater setup each night:
- **Night 1 (Dec 17):** *The Foundations* – An acoustic and live big-band journey through Davido's early catalogue from *Omo Baba Olowo* to *A Good Time*.
- **Night 2 (Dec 18):** *Timeless & Beyond* – A celebration of *Timeless* and his recent global chart-toppers with full brass and orchestral backing.
- **Night 3 (Dec 19):** *The All-Star Gala* – A star-studded finale featuring surprise guest performances from across the African continent and diaspora.

### Economic and Cultural Impact

The residency is expected to draw thousands of international tourists and diaspora travelers to Lagos for the kickoff of Detty December. 

Tickets are slated to go on sale via a specialized verified-fan lottery system designed to combat ticket scalping and ensure local Nigerian fans have fair access.

Davido's National Theatre residency promises to be the defining cultural event of the 2026 holiday calendar.
