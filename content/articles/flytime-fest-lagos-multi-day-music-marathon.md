For nearly two decades, Cecil Hammond’s Flytime Promotions has stood as the premier architect of live entertainment in Nigeria. And every December, the crowning jewel of the festive calendar is **Flytime Fest**, hosted at the prestigious Eko Convention Centre in Victoria Island, Lagos.

Spanning four consecutive nights of non-stop music, Flytime Fest combines arena-grade technical production with the glamour and raw energy of Lagos nightlife.

### Four Nights of Unmatched Star Power

The festival's multi-day format offers a meticulously curated experience:
- **Day 1: Rhythm Unplugged:** The legendary opening night featuring a rapid-fire marathon of over 25 artists and comedians, bridging veteran legends like 2Baba and P-Square with the hottest breakout acts of the year.
- **Days 2 & 3: Headliner Showcases:** Dedicated solo arena-level headline concerts by superstars like Asake, Kizz Daniel, and Olamide, complete with bespoke stage sets and extended live band arrangements.
- **Day 4: The Grand Finale:** A star-studded closing party featuring international headliners alongside Afrobeats royalty.

### The Standard in Production Excellence

In a region where live concerts have historically faced logistical hurdles—sound delays, power fluctuations, and crowd control issues—Flytime Fest has established a reputation for flawless execution.

Featuring world-class line-array sound systems, cutting-edge pyrotechnics, and synchronized LED wristbands for attendees, the festival delivers an experience on par with Coachella or the Wireless Festival.

### The Ultimate Networking Hub

The festival’s VIP tables have become the ultimate annual gathering place for African tech founders, venture capitalists, Nollywood actors, sports icons, and global diaspora elites.

Flytime Fest is more than a concert series; it is the annual summit of African creative and commercial excellence.
