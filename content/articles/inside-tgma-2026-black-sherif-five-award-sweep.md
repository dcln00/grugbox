The Grand Arena at the Accra International Conference Centre was electric on Saturday, May 9, 2026, as Ghana's biggest musical night—the 27th Telecel Ghana Music Awards (TGMA)—unfolded in spectacular fashion.

From jaw-dropping red-carpet haute couture to surprise live reunions, the evening belonged unequivocally to Mohammed Ismail Sharrif, known globally as **Black Sherif**, who swept five major categories including the coveted **Artiste of the Year**.

### A Red-Carpet Extravaganza

Long before the first statuette was presented, the TGMA red carpet delivered an unforgettable fashion showcase. Ghanaian designers dominated the step-and-repeat: hand-woven Kente tuxedos, avant-garde structural gowns inspired by Ga fishing nets, and upcycled denim statements from Accra's underground streetwear scene.

Host Berla Mundi anchored the live broadcast with signature poise, guiding viewers through four hours of dazzling live television.

### Blacko's Five-Award Triumphant Night

When Black Sherif took to the stage to collect **Artiste of the Year**—securing the top honor for the second time in his remarkable career—the arena erupted in deafening cheers. His trophy haul for the evening included:
- **Artiste of the Year**
- **Hiphop Song of the Year** (*Where Dem Boys*)
- **Afropop Song of the Year** (*Sacrifice*)
- **Best Music Video**
- **Songwriter of the Year**

"This is for every kid in Konongo Zongo who was told their dreams were too big," an emotional Blacko said during his acceptance speech, holding the gold trophy aloft.

### Unforgettable Live Performances

The evening's live sets were among the strongest in TGMA history:
- **Sarkodie** delivered a blistering 15-minute medley celebrating two decades of hip-hop dominance.
- **Stonebwoy** brought the house down with an electrifying, high-energy live band rendition of "Jejereje".
- **Kofi Kinaata** serenaded the crowd with Highlife Song of the Year winner "It is Finished", joined by a 20-piece traditional brass orchestra.
- **Medikal**, who took home four awards including Best Hiplife/Hip-Hop Artiste and Best Collaboration (*Shoulder*), surprised fans with a high-energy surprise set.

TGMA 2026 was a resounding celebration of Ghanaian musical resilience, proof that Accra remains one of the world's most vibrant musical capitals.
