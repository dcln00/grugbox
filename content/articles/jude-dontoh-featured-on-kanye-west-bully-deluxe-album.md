**Jude Dontoh**, a Ghanaian fashion designer and creative director who co-founded Tribe of God, has vocals on **“I CAN’T WAIT”**, a **Kanye West** record featuring **Ms. Lauryn Hill**. The track is on the deluxe edition of ***BULLY***.

Dontoh is also Creative Director for Ms. Lauryn Hill, and he added vocal textures to the record, a collaboration that spans Accra, Chicago and South Orange.
