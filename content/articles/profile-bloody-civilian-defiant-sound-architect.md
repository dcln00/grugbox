In an industry where female artists are often pressured into rigid pop archetypes and rarely credited behind the mixing console, Emoseh Khamofu—known as **Bloody Civilian**—is an exhilarating shock to the system.

Hailing from Taraba State in northern Nigeria, the singer, songwriter, and visionary producer crafts music that is furious, witty, eccentric, and completely addictive.

### From Kaduna to the Marvel Universe

Bloody Civilian first sent shockwaves through the industry with her self-produced track "How To Kill A Man", a blistering blend of electro-folk, dark humor, and cinematic drama. 

Her singular sound caught the attention of soundtrack curators for Marvel's *Black Panther: Wakanda Forever*, where she was tapped to co-write and produce "Wake Up" alongside Afropop heavyweight Rema.

Her debut EP, *Anger Management* (2023), followed by its high-profile remix edition featuring Tay Iwar, Odumodublvck, and Bella Shmurda, established her as one of the sharpest conceptual minds in modern African music.

### The Total Creative Controller

What sets Bloody Civilian apart is her absolute command over every stage of her creative output. She writes her own lyrics, arranges her own vocal stacks, builds her own beats from scratch, and directs her own visual concepts.

Her sound is characterized by unorthodox drum patterns, distorted bass drops, and sharp, satirical lyrics tackling patriarchal hypocrisy, mental health, and social frustration.

### Redefining African Pop Possibilities

"I chose the name Bloody Civilian because I'm not a soldier, but I've lived through the everyday battles of being an African woman," she explains. 

With her boundless ambition and fearless sonic experimentation, Bloody Civilian is proving that the future of African pop belongs to the bold.
