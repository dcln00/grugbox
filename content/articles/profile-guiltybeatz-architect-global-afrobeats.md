Behind some of the most influential African and global pop anthems of the last decade sits a quiet, soft-spoken genius in East Legon, Accra: Ronald Banful, known professionally as **GuiltyBeatz**.

While casual listeners may know him as the producer behind Mr Eazi's viral dance anthem "Akwaaba", global music insiders revere him as one of the most versatile and forward-thinking producers on the planet.

### The Beyoncé Collaborations: *The Lion King* and *Renaissance*

GuiltyBeatz’s international career reached stratospheric heights when he was recruited by pop icon Beyoncé for *The Lion King: The Gift* (2019), producing standouts like "Already" (featuring Shatta Wale), "Find Your Way Back", and "Keys to the Kingdom".

He followed this triumph by contributing production to Beyoncé’s critically acclaimed, Grammy-winning electronic opus *Renaissance* (2022), earning him multiple Grammy nominations and cementing his reputation as a master of four-on-the-floor dance grooves.

### Bridging African Rhythms and Global Electronic Music

What makes GuiltyBeatz unique is his refusal to be pigeonholed. Whether blending Ghanaian highlife guitar riffs with UK garage syncopation, or fusing South African Gqom with Detroit techno, his productions are characterized by pristine spatial mixing, dynamic basslines, and intricate percussion layering.

His solo EPs, including *Different* and recent collaborations with European electronic tastemakers, demonstrate a producer continually expanding the sonic frontiers of African electronic music.

### Mentoring the Next Generation

GuiltyBeatz has also dedicated substantial time to mentoring young sound engineers and producers across West Africa. Through studio workshops and open-source sample packs, he is actively nurturing the next wave of Ghanaian sonic pioneers.

In the pantheon of West African producers, GuiltyBeatz stands as a peerless architect of timeless global sound.
