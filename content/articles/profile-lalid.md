Tema has produced Sarkodie, R2Bees and Kwesi Arthur, which means every rapper who comes out of the harbour city gets measured against that list the moment they pick up a mic. **Lalid** treats it as useful pressure rather than a burden.

His name is an acronym: Living A Life I Deserve. He chose it in junior high school, which sounds like something you'd grow out of, except he never did.

### _The Matter_, and what came after

His national radio introduction was _The Matter_, a record that got picked up fast on social media and in clubs and eventually earned him an official remix with Medikal. Plenty of artists would have chased that sound for another two years. He didn't.

What separates him is pace. Over Afro-fusion and street-hop production he tends to sit back in the pocket rather than sprint through it, and it makes the everyday-money rhymes in _Catching Glory_, _Suffer_ and _Sit Up_ land harder than they would at double speed. In 2026 he held his own on _I_, a Kelvyn Boy album cut with Strongman on it, which is a fair test of where he actually stands. Nominations at the Ghana Music Awards and Ghana Music Awards UK followed, along with a reputation as a live act worth turning up for.

### LALID LIVE and the longer bet

A lot of young artists with momentum spend it on playlist placements. Lalid is spending some of his on a live show series, **LALID LIVE**, which is a slower and less glamorous way to build a career and probably a smarter one. The point is a concert circuit that survives past whichever trend is current.

He describes music in Tema as closer to a survival code than entertainment, and says the goal is a platform that outlives any single trend, with international festival headline slots at the end of it.
