Whenever the ominous audio tag *"Telz! Telz! Born Killer!"* rings out over a booming sub-bass, listeners around the world know they are in for an auditory masterclass.

Alli Odunayo, professionally known as **Telz**, is one of the most accomplished and visionary music producers operating in West Africa today. His work on Burna Boy's Grammy-winning album *Twice As Tall* (2020) and subsequent blockbuster *Love, Damini* (2022) elevated him into the elite pantheon of African record producers.

### The Organic Symphony of Telz

What distinguishes a Telz production from generic algorithmic beats is his deep commitment to live, dynamic instrumentation. Rather than relying solely on repetitive digital loops, Telz frequently incorporates live saxophone sections, authentic Bata talking drums, and soaring choral harmonies.

His work on monumental tracks like Burna Boy's "Wonderful", "Onyeka (Baby)", and "Wetin Dey Sup" demonstrated a rare ability to fuse indigenous African folk rhythms with stadium-sized pop sensibilities.

### Expanding Collaborative Horizons

Beyond his historic partnership with Burna Boy, Telz has shaped hit records for an illustrious roster including Patoranking, Buju (BNXN), Crayon, and international collaborators. 

His production style is marked by dramatic dynamic shifts—building from intimate acoustic intros to explosive, horn-drenched climaxes that demand movement.

### The Producer as Cultural Custodian

"As an African producer, you are holding the sonic memory of your ancestors in your hands," Telz states. "Every drum pattern you lay down is connected to centuries of tradition. Our job is to carry that memory into the future."

With a Grammy statuette already in his cabinet and dozens of multi-platinum singles to his name, Telz is engineering the global soundtrack of West African excellence.
