Grugbox is the premier independent destination documenting modern African music, creative culture, and youth movements. From Alté and Afrobeats to Highlife, Amapiano, and diaspora hip-hop, our publication commands a loyal, tastemaking readership of music creators, cultural innovators, and dedicated listeners.

Partnering with Grugbox gives brands authentic access to cultural conversations that shape contemporary West Africa and its global diaspora.

## 1. Our Readership & Influence

Our audience consists of passionate music consumers, young urban professionals, creatives, and industry decision-makers:

- **Core Markets:** Accra, Lagos, London, New York, Toronto, Johannesburg, and Berlin.
- **Audience Profile:** 18–35 demographic with high cultural engagement, active streaming habits, and strong affinity for contemporary fashion, live events, nightlife, and technology.
- **Engagement:** High time-on-page driven by long-form cultural essays, rigorous album reviews, and curated playlists.

## 2. Partnership Opportunities

We work collaboratively with select brands, festivals, record labels, and cultural institutions to create high-impact campaigns:

### Display Advertising (IAB Standard Units)
- **Top Leaderboard (728x90 / Mobile 320x50):** Premium high-visibility placement below navigation across desktop and mobile.
- **In-Article MPU (300x250 / 336x280):** High-engagement contextual display integrated into popular review and interview pages.
- **Bottom Banner (728x90):** Wide leaderboard placement above footer sections.

### Sponsored Features & Brand Spotlights
- **Custom Cultural Essays:** Long-form editorial storytelling produced in collaboration with our creative strategists, highlighting cultural moments, product launches, or creative milestones.
- **Visual Profiles:** Photo and video stories profiling forward-thinking artists, designers, or entrepreneurs supported by your brand.
- *Note: All sponsored stories are transparently labeled with "Sponsored Feature" or "Presented in Partnership With [Brand]".*

### Newsletter Sponsorships (The Grugbox Digest)
- **Dedicated Sponsor Takeovers:** Exclusive header banner, brand introduction, and custom call-to-action in our Friday weekly digest delivered directly to subscriber inboxes.
- **Sponsored Curation:** Presenting sponsor of our weekly new release roundup and playlist links.

### Event & Festival Partnerships
- **Grugbox Live Accra:** Co-branded live performances, panel discussions, listening parties, and DJ sets in Accra.
- **Festival Activations:** Curated stages, media lounges, and exclusive backstage interviews at premier African music festivals.

### Curated Streaming Playlists
- **Official Playlist Sponsorship:** Brand integration across Grugbox's Apple Music and Spotify playlists (including *Nightfall Accra*, *Alté & Highlife*, and *Grugbox Essentials*).

## 3. Strict Separation of Editorial and Advertising

Grugbox maintains strict journalistic independence:

- **Reviews and Ratings are Never for Sale:** Review scores, critical verdicts, and editorial inclusion cannot be purchased, negotiated, or influenced by advertising partnerships.
- **Transparent Labeling:** Readers always know when content is commercial. All paid, sponsored, or branded collaborations carry unmistakable labeling in compliance with international advertising standards.
- We only partner with brands whose ethos respects our audience and creative community.

## 4. Technical Specs & Creative Guidelines

All display advertising assets and sponsored assets must meet Grugbox technical requirements:

- **Formats Accepted:** PNG, JPG, GIF, HTML5, WebM / MP4 (for video features).
- **Max File Size:** 150KB (standard display), 5MB (high-resolution hero imagery).
- **Delivery Timeline:** Standard digital creative must be received at least 3 business days prior to campaign launch; custom content requires 10 business days lead time.
