At Grugbox, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you browse our site, subscribe to our newsletters, or contact us, and how that information is handled.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two principal ways: information you provide voluntarily, and information collected automatically through standard web protocols.

### Information You Provide Directly
- **Newsletter Subscription Data:** When you sign up for the Grugbox Digest or edition alerts, we collect your email address, optional first name, and topic preferences.
- **Direct Correspondence:** If you contact us via email at `info@grugbox.com`, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message.
- **Reader Surveys & Contests:** When you participate in editorial surveys, giveaways, or live event RSVPs, we may collect requested contact details.

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Log Data:** Like most websites, our servers automatically record data such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and access timestamps.
- **Device & Interaction Analytics:** We use non-invasive analytics (e.g. Google Analytics via `nuxt-gtag`) to measure readership metrics, popular reviews, bounce rates, and geographic traffic trends.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to deliver and enhance the Grugbox reading experience:

- **Newsletter Dispatches:** Delivering weekly reviews, curated playlist links, editorial roundups, and special cultural coverage you selected.
- **Site Performance & Optimization:** Analyzing reader traffic to improve page load speed, optimize mobile performance, and identify popular topics.
- **Editorial Communication:** Responding directly to reader feedback, music submissions, interview requests, and corrections.
- **Security & Integrity:** Monitoring and preventing malicious traffic, spam submissions, or unauthorized scraping attempts.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal contact information to third-party data brokers.

## 3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Grugbox uses cookies and local storage to preserve essential site functionality:

- **Essential Storage:** Remembering your visual preferences (such as drawer states, read articles, or notification dismissals).
- **Analytics Cookies:** Aggregated, pseudonymized measurement of audience visits to help our editorial team understand which artists and genres resonate most with readers.
- You can configure your browser to decline cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. Note that certain interactive features of the site may function with minor limitations if cookies are disabled.

## 4. Third-Party Embeds and External Services

Grugbox articles often include embedded media players and external widgets, including:

- **Music & Video Streaming:** Embedded players from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and Audiomack.
- **Image Optimization & Delivery:** Images delivered via Cloudinary and local IPX image processors.
- **Content Management:** Administrative systems connected via secure GitHub proxies.

These third-party providers may collect data according to their own privacy policies when you interact with embedded players. We encourage you to review their respective policies.

## 5. Data Security and Retention

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. We retain subscriber data only for as long as your newsletter subscription remains active. If you unsubscribe, your email is promptly removed from our active distribution lists.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Regardless of your geographic location, Grugbox respects your control over your data:

- **Unsubscribe:** Every newsletter email includes an instant "Unsubscribe" link at the footer. Clicking it immediately halts further scheduled mailings.
- **Access and Correction:** You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you or request corrections by emailing `info@grugbox.com`.
- **Right to Deletion:** You may request complete erasure of your subscriber record at any time.

## 7. International Data Considerations

Grugbox is headquartered in Accra, Ghana, and operates globally. By using Grugbox, you acknowledge that your information may be processed on servers located internationally where privacy regulations may differ from those in your local jurisdiction.

## 8. Updates to this Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our editorial practices or legal obligations. When updates occur, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will be revised.
