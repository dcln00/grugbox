**Grugbox Essentials: Alté** is a sonic deep-dive into the vanguard of Nigerian and West African alternative expression. Born as a rebellious counterculture against commercial radio rigidity, the Alté movement has evolved into one of the continent's most daring creative ecosystems—a boundary-less space where artists construct intimate, non-linear worlds using eccentric visual language and fearless genre experimentation.

The playlist captures the genre's multifaceted sonic palette, weaving together moody lo-fi textures, hazy R&B melodies, woozy trap-infused percussion, and warm retro highlife chord progressions. Featuring pivotal cuts and underground essentials from visionaries across Lagos, Accra, and the wider diaspora, this collection celebrates artists who prioritize emotional vulnerability, analog character, and creative freedom above all else.
