**Rating:** 8.8 / 10  
**Release Date:** May 1, 2026  
**Label:** YBNL Nation / EMPIRE  
**Key Tracks:** _Worship_ (with DJ Snake), _Badman Gangsta_ (feat. Tiakola), _Asambe_ (feat. Kabza De Small)

When Ahmed Ololade, known globally as **Asake**, burst into mainstream prominence in 2022, sceptics questioned whether his high-energy fusion of Fuji street chants, dramatic string flourishes, and amapiano log drums could sustain longevity. Four albums in four years later, Asake has not only silenced doubters—he has fundamentally re-engineered the sonic architecture of West African popular music.

His fourth studio album, **_M$NEY_**, is a 13-track tour de force that sees the Lagos-born superstar push his sonic boundaries into uncharted electronic, dance, and global club territories without sacrificing an ounce of his Yoruba spiritual roots.

### Sonic Expansion Meets Spiritual Grounding

From the opening moments of "Worship"—a collaboration with French electronic powerhouse DJ Snake—Asake establishes that this is an album built for international festival main stages. The track marries cathedral-sized organ chords with thumping electro-house percussion, over which Asake delivers an electrifying prayer of thanksgiving that feels both club-ready and deeply sacred.

On "Badman Gangsta", featuring French-Congolese rapper Tiakola, Asake navigates Parisian Afro-trap rhythms with seamless ease, his rhythmic cadence trading bars in a flawless bilingual dance. Meanwhile, on "Asambe", he reunites with the King of Amapiano, Kabza De Small, delivering a hypnotic, late-night deep house groove layered with intricate Zulu and Yoruba vocal chants.

### The Verdict

Where lesser artists might dilute their identity when chasing global crossover appeal, Asake doubles down on what made him singular. His choral harmonies remain dense and impassioned; his lyrical references to street perseverance and Divine grace are as heartfelt as ever. **_M$NEY_** is the sound of an artist operating at the absolute pinnacle of his powers.
