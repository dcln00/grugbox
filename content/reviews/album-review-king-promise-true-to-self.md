Gregory Bortey Newman, known across the globe as **King Promise**, possesses one of the most instantly recognizable, honeyed voices in modern African music. His third studio album, ***True to Self***—which took home Album of the Year at the 2025 Telecel Ghana Music Awards—stands as his most accomplished body of work to date.

### The Art of the Timeless Melody

Where many modern projects prioritize frenetic tempo, King Promise understands the timeless power of lush melodies and patient vocal delivery. 

The smash hit "Paris" remains a masterclass in romantic Afropop: warm basslines, shimmering Spanish-influenced guitar chords, and King Promise's effortless falsetto. On "Favourite Story", he crafts a glorious homage to vintage Ghanaian Highlife, sampling Ofori Amponsah's classic "Otoolege" while teaming up with rap heavyweight Sarkodie and rising sensation Olivetheboy.

On "Continental", he links up with Nigerian street phenomenon Shallipopi, seamlessly blending his refined vocal smoothness with Benin City street swagger.

### Cultural Authenticity with Global Appeal

Executive produced alongside frequent collaborator Killbeatz, *True to Self* strikes an exquisite balance between international polish and Ghanaian cultural pride. Ga language idioms, Twi romantic proverbs, and English top-lines weave together naturally.

***True to Self*** is an undeniable victory for Ghanaian music—an album that proves soulful, melody-first songwriting will always transcend borders.
