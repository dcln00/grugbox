In modern West African hip-hop, consistency is often preached. Few practice it the way Kweku Smoke does.

While his peers agonize over multi-year rollout strategies, brand alignments and algorithmic TikTok trends, Smoke operates like a blue-collar tradesman who clocks into the booth every single morning. Arriving hot on the heels of 2025's acclaimed _Walk With Me_, **_No Days Off_** marks the rapper's seventh full-length studio album. Seven cohesive, culturally impactful albums in a scene where many struggle to deliver two.

Released on September 4, 2026, the 13-track record is the definitive statement of an artist whose entire career is anchored in grit, self-reliance and spiritual perseverance.

## The gospel of the hustle

The phrase "No Days Off" has become a tired motivational hashtag in Western corporate parlance, but in Kweku Smoke's hands, it is stripped of all that gloss and restored to its raw, unforgiving reality. For urban youth across Accra, Kumasi and beyond, taking a day off isn't about self-care. It is the terrifying difference between eating and going hungry, between keeping a roof overhead and being swallowed by the streets.

Smoke has always possessed a rare gift for articulating that pressure without romanticizing the suffering. He understands the psychological cost of constant survival. On _No Days Off_, he holds up a mirror to the Ghanaian everyday grind: the mechanics, the traders, the dreamers risking everything abroad, and the creative independents who refuse to kiss industry rings. He carries the bulk of this record entirely on his own shoulders, allowing carefully chosen guest verses from **Lasmid**, **Kwesi Arthur** and **Dikoo** to read as comrades rather than commercial bait.

## The tracklist

![No Days Off Official Tracklist Artwork](/content/kweku-smoke-no-days-off-tracklist.jpg)

### 1. Soul Healer & 2. Hallelujah

Smoke makes a striking artistic decision by refusing to open with aggressive, trap-heavy gunfire. Instead, _No Days Off_ begins on its knees. _Soul Healer_ and _Hallelujah_ form a sacred, contemplative entry point. Over gentle keys and atmospheric chords, Smoke searches for internal tranquility, thanking God for shielding him through backstabs and label minefields. The record's premise is plain: before you can endure the streets, your spirit must be aligned.

### 3. Okwantunii & 4. Survival

"Okwantunii", the Akan word for _The Traveler_ or _Migrant_, is one of the most haunting and deeply affecting records Smoke has ever penned. Pairing naturally with _Survival_, these tracks tackle the heavy, unspoken burdens carried by young Ghanaians who leave home in pursuit of greener pastures. Smoke captures the biting loneliness, the cultural displacement, and the crushing expectation of sending money back home when your own pockets are bleeding.

### 5. Bra & 6. Mmereniho

Shifting toward emotional vulnerability, _Bra_ and _Mmereniho_ dive into personal relationships and the passage of time. Smoke grapples with the people who faded away the moment the road became rocky, contrasting them with the loyal few who stayed when the lights were off. The production here leans into highlife guitars and melancholic basslines, providing a resonant bed for his husky, reflective cadence.

### 7. Power House & 8. Bombele

For the diehards craving the speaker-rattling street heat that built Kweku Smoke's underground following, this two-track stretch is absolute combustion. With distorted 808s and sinister hi-hat rolls, Smoke slips into his most menacing pocket. On _Bombele_, his flow does not let up, and the reminder lands: his soul has matured, but his street pedigree remains untamable.

### 9. Life Of A Hustler & 10. Set It Straight

These tracks are the philosophical spine of the second half. _Life Of A Hustler_ details the daily mechanics of survival, while _Set It Straight_ addresses industry chatter, counterfeit friendships and creative sovereignty head-on. Alongside a potent feature from Dikoo, Smoke sets the record straight: respect is earned through labor, not social media noise.

### 11. NO DAYS OFF

The thunderous title track arrives like a siren. Propelled by urgent percussion and a delivery that does not let up, Smoke encapsulates his entire life's code into under four minutes. There are no shortcuts, no silver spoons, and no room for complacency. It is an adrenaline shot for anyone grinding in the dark.

### 12. Through The Pain & 13. Faith (feat. Lasmid)

The finale of _No Days Off_ delivers an unforgettable emotional payoff. _Through The Pain_ sets the stage with bruised, battle-tested resilience, which cascades into the lead single, _Faith_.

Teaming up with **Lasmid**, whose vocal hook on the chorus carries the song, the track achieves a real synergy. Lasmid's soulful, soaring melodies elevate Smoke's gravelly verses into a stadium-sized anthem of hope. It is the perfect conclusion: a reminder that the sweat, the tears and the hustle were never in vain.

## The crown of consistency

Seven studio albums in, Kweku Smoke occupies a singular tier in Ghanaian music. He doesn't seek permission from mainstream gatekeepers, nor does he dilute his linguistic identity to chase ephemeral cross-border fads.

**_No Days Off_** is raw, spiritual and defiant. It is the sound of a street prophet at the absolute height of his creative powers, proving that while talent may turn heads, it is steady, uninterrupted work that builds dynasties.
