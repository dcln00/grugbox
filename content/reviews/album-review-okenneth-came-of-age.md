When the Asakaa wave broke out of Kumasi in 2020, O'Kenneth was on the front line as its most distinctive, brooding voice. With his gravelly baritone, menacing cadence and dark cinematic sensibility, he was the anchor of the sound.

The problem for any underground wave is survival after the hype settles. On **_CAME OF AGE_**, released on May 1, 2026, O'Kenneth answers that question. Across 18 tracks the Kumerican heavyweight does not abandon drill. He pushes it into a form that can carry the weight of adulthood, loss, brotherhood and spiritual reconciliation.

## Maturing in the smoke

The title is not an empty boast. After overnight fame, shifting friendships and the pressure to provide for a whole neighborhood, O'Kenneth has watched youthful bravado harden into wisdom. Where early Asakaa anthems ran on adrenaline and turf, _CAME OF AGE_ looks inward.

O'Kenneth strips back his armor to examine the scars he picked up along the way. The production mixes drill slides with melancholic pianos and bluesy electric guitars, and it sounds like Kumasi after midnight: quiet and reflective, with danger underneath.

## The tracklist

![CAME OF AGE Official Tracklist Artwork](/content/okenneth-came-of-age-tracklist.jpg)

### 1. EASY & 2. MENTUMI NKA

The album opens with _EASY_ and _MENTUMI NKA_, and sets its cinematic tone straight away. Over atmospheric choral hums and slow-burning percussion, O'Kenneth rides a laid-back groove and dismisses industry pretenders without raising his voice.

### 4. PAIN II & 5. COLD SHOULDER

The emotional centerpiece of the album's first half. In _PAIN II_ Kenneth's delivery is heavy with grief as he works through betrayal from trusted circles. _COLD SHOULDER_ answers with an icy, drill-laced tempo, and it captures the emotional defenses an artist builds when everyone around him suddenly expects a handout.

### 7. FAR FROM HOME (w/ Kwaku DMC)

Teaming up with longtime Asakaa brother **Kwaku DMC**, _FAR FROM HOME_ is the best display of chemistry on the album. The two reflect on playing international stages while staying tethered to the dusty streets of Kumasi. DMC's laid-back, kingly presence sits well against Kenneth's urgent rasp.

### 10. OTOTROBONSU & 11. ME MPAEBƆ (feat. 7enR)

_ME MPAEBƆ_, Akan for _My Prayers_, is a moment of spiritual grounding with **7enR**, where Kenneth thanks his mother and ancestors for shielding him from street traps. It follows the traditional percussion of _OTOTROBONSU_.

### 13. CAME OF AGE (Interlude) & 16. BEETLES (feat. Chicogod)

The album's thesis lands on the title interlude, _CAME OF AGE_, which weighs the boy who entered the storm against the man who survived it. _BEETLES_ reconnects with **Chicogod** for a moshpit-ready reminder that when O'Kenneth wants to rap circles around the competition, nobody in the country can touch him.

### 18. BALENCIAGA

_BALENCIAGA_ closes the record with a look at luxury consumerism and industry vanity. Kenneth leaves the mic with the calm of someone who has been through it.

## The verdict

_CAME OF AGE_ is a milestone for O'Kenneth and for Ghanaian hip-hop. It proves the Asakaa wave was never a gimmick. It was the incubator for some of the most versatile, deeply expressive voices of this generation.

O'Kenneth has traded raw anger for wisdom. _CAME OF AGE_ is a battle-tested record, and it puts him among West Africa's essential rappers.
