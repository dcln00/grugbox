Crown Uzama, widely known as **Shallipopi** or the 'Pluto President', is one of the most unique cultural phenomena to emerge from Nigeria in recent years. Hailing from Benin City, Edo State, Shallipopi bypassed conventional Afrobeats melodies to construct an entirely distinct sub-genre: relaxed, hypnotic, conversational street-hop propelled by heavy basslines and rich Edo linguistic idioms.

His third studio album, ***Auracle***, proves that his viral success was no fluke; it is the work of a calculated stylistic genius.

### The Hypnotic Power of Understatement

Where most street-hop artists rely on shouting energy and frantic pacing, Shallipopi's genius lies in his supreme nonchalance. He raps as though he is having a relaxed conversation in a Benin City lounge, yet his rhythmic pockets are razor-sharp.

On tracks like "Pluto Oracle" and "Benin Boy Global", he pairs heavy, distorted 808 sub-bass with sparkling keyboard melodies, sprinkling tracks with viral catchphrases like 'Evian', 'Pluto', and 'Clear'. 

### Ancient Heritage in Modern Guise

Beneath the playful party lyrics, *Auracle* is steeped in Benin cultural folklore. Shallipopi frequently invokes traditional Edo proverbs, references the historic Benin Kingdom, and credits his ancestors with his meteoric rise.

***Auracle*** is infectious, unapologetically local, and endlessly entertaining. Shallipopi has built his own planet, and the entire continent is gladly living on it.
