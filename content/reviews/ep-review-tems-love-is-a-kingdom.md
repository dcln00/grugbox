**Rating:** 9.0 / 10  
**Release Date:** November 21, 2025  
**Label:** Since '93 / RCA Records  
**Key Tracks:** *Kingdom*, *Sanctuary*, *Hold On To Light*

Temilade Openiyi—**Tems**—occupies a unique category in modern music. Possessing a rich, resonant contralto and an intuitive grasp of harmonic architecture, she creates music that feels like a sacred sanctuary amidst the noise of contemporary pop.

Her 7-track EP, ***Love Is A Kingdom***, serves as an intimate bridge leading into her highly anticipated sophomore studio album. It is a breathtaking exhibition of artistic autonomy, spiritual exploration, and vocal mastery.

### Self-Produced Sonic Sanctum

Tems produced or co-produced five of the seven tracks on the project, and her sonic fingerprints are unmistakable: spacious reverb, warm live basslines, understated acoustic guitars, and multi-tracked vocal choirs that evoke both gospel transcendence and classic 1970s soul.

The title track, "Kingdom", opens with an ethereal vocal drone before blooming into an intimate meditation on divine unconditional love. On "Sanctuary", she explores the quiet sanctuary found in self-acceptance, her vocals soaring effortlessly into a poignant head voice.

### The Power of Stillness

In an era dominated by high-tempo party tracks, *Love Is A Kingdom* is a radical act of stillness. Tems does not rush; every note is given room to breathe, every pause carries emotional weight.

***Love Is A Kingdom*** is a masterwork of modern African soul, proving once again that Tems is an artist who marches exclusively to the rhythm of her own spirit.
